# Intake and research before the audit (Daniel, Oct 29 16:01, D-077)

Every unit, mini-program or system step runs INTAKE → RESEARCH → AUDIT → PLAN → execution → RETRO. Incident (Oct 29): the request was "gate via API", the real intention "PRs enter on their own and fast, toward 1,000 PRs/day"; we rebuilt a strict train and only afterwards found that Uber (SubmitQueue), Google (TAP/Bazel) and the GitHub/GitLab merge queues already solve it.

1. INTAKE, `docs/units/<unit>/intake.md` (template `docs/process/templates/intake.md`): the adapter that turns an external request into our understanding. Sections `## Pedido` (the request, literal, not interpreted), `## Intenção` (the real why), `## Contexto` (current state, deadlines, what came before, dependencies), `## Regras` (D-NNN, premises, Padrão rules that apply), `## Problema real` (one sentence + the metric of "resolved"), `## Lacunas` (what I do not know and on whom it depends; if only the requester knows, ask before spending effort).
2. RESEARCH, `docs/units/<unit>/research.md` (template `docs/process/templates/research.md`): looks outward, starting from the intake's real problem. Sections `## Quem já resolveu` (3–5 real references, link and what each solves), `## Estado da arte` (the standard solution, the most advanced, what each requires), `## Encaixe em nós` (copy directly / adapt / does not fit, with the reason), `## Escada de ambição` (the minimum useful step now → the path to the state of the art; its first rung is the plan's `## Cheapest useful effort`). Short: about one page and 15 minutes of session; an obvious reference takes one line.
3. Decoupling: each step delivers its complete, independent product and never dictates what the next one does. The research raises no questions for the audit; the audit builds its own. The next step only reads the previous product: the AUDIT reads intake.md and research.md as inputs, names them in its `## Entradas` section, and measures our current code against the research's reference in `## Distância do estado da arte` (audit-and-plan.md, template `docs/process/templates/audit.md`).

Order at launch: branch and draft PR first (STEP 0), then intake.md, research.md, audit.md, plan.md as their own commits, then code. A program (`docs/programs/<program>/`) carries the same two files. The `structure check` stage refuses a unit or program folder first committed after `intake.since` (`config/registry/intake.json`) without both files or with a section missing or empty, and, once its audit.md exists, an audit without `## Entradas` (naming both files) or `## Distância do estado da arte` (`scripts/lib/unit_intake.py`); older folders are not judged.
