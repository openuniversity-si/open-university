# Retro before merge (Daniel 00:24)

This is quota well spent, do it thoroughly. After the final gate passes and before you merge, commit docs/units/<unit>/retro.md and post it as one PR comment titled "RETRO". It covers the WHOLE unit, from the initial brief to the accepted gate: (1) brief and intake quality: what was missing, ambiguous or wrong, and whether the intake's real problem held; (2) research, audit and plan: whether the research changed the plan, whether the audit's root causes held (a later failure from a cause the chains missed is a root the audit stopped short of), whether the chosen alternative was the right level, which premises held or broke, how the plan changed and why; (3) execution: every failure with its root-cause class, rework, dead ends, waits, and where time and quota went (timeline with commit times); (4) gate: how many runs, what failed each time, known defects hit; (5) lessons: for each problem, the concrete change to a rule, brief template, config, tool or shared helper that would have prevented it, and who owns it. Facts with evidence, no filler. Never skip it.
