# Decide, then report (Daniel, Oct 26 2026, mandatory)

Never stop and wait for an owner decision. When you hit a choice, pick the safest reversible option that satisfies the rules (never force-push, never rewrite pushed history, never loosen thresholds or timeouts, never touch production or secrets), act on it at once, and record the decision and its reason in a PR comment. Only these are not yours to take, and even for them you keep working on everything else: spending money, production, source-of-truth changes. Ending a turn with "this is a decision for the owner" is a failure.
