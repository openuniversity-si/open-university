# gate-speed — F7: ≤ 60 s

Phase 7 of the [solução](../solucao.md) (D-122, D-125: the CPU the tests spend). F5 left the suite at 94.9 s cold
and 310.5 CPU-s ([F5](F5.md)); F6 measured that it is bound by its CPU ([F6](F6.md)).

## Auditoria
Measured on `6355f2de` in the session container: every harness process logging its CPU and its children's at exit,
grouped by each script test's private TMPDIR, then `strace -f -e trace=execve` of `train.test.sh`.
1. **The train's tests are the largest share of the CPU:** `train.test.sh` 56.8 CPU-s (20.0 of it Python),
   `train-reuse` 14.7, `train-role` 10.1, about a quarter of the suite; then the structure check's tests 24.5,
   `generate` 17.3, `config` 14.2, `merge-regenerate` 12.5.
2. **Half of the train tests' processes are the local transport.** `train.test.sh` starts 9,800 processes; 307
   pushes of the fixture (one base push in every setUp, one per PR it makes) each start seven (`push`, `sh`,
   `receive-pack`, `pack-objects`, `unpack-objects`, `rev-list`, `gc --auto`), plus the fetches that follow. The
   fixture's origin and seed live on the same disk; a push copies objects the origin could read in place. The system
   that fails: a fixture publishes a ref through the machinery the train's tests are not about (the train pushes
   nothing itself: merging is the forge's job, the fixture's fake forge).
