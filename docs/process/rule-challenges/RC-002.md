# RC-002 — serial first caps the metric at about 216 commits per day
- Grade: 2
- Status: open
- Rule: "Grow capacity progressively: first serial commits from this single session, then parallel, then dynamic, then continuous; do not jump to launching many parallel sessions now" (Daniel, 31/10 13:28 BRT, FLUXO-AMPLO).
- Evidence: audit findings 3 and 4 (`docs/units/FLUXO-AMPLO/audit.md`): a serial lane spends 70 % of its cycle authoring (33.5 of 47.9 min per 5-commit PR, n = 46 PRs since 29/10) and 30 % waiting on gate and train; the lane's commits per hour are n/(a·n + W) with a = 6.7 min and W = 14.4 min, at most 9 per hour, 216 per day. Phase 1's sub-phases 300 to 1,000 per day cannot be reached by one serial lane unless authoring per commit falls below 4.5 min.
- Harm: from sub-phase 300 on, the rule blocks the main metric; the serial stage will be exhausted as soon as sub-phase 200 is measured.
- Proposal: keep serial work (F1.1 and F1.2) as ordered, and lift the "not parallel yet" part at the moment a full lane-day is measured at 200 counted commits per day or authoring per commit is measured at 4.5 min or less; then start two lanes (F1.3), never more than the train and the guards allow.
- Impact: costs session quota (a second lane), touches no production and no other people; reversible by stopping the lane. The quota cost is the part the watchman may want to take to Daniel.
