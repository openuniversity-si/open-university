- Grade: 2
- Status: upheld
- Rule: "Tier 2 runs the gate on the batch's combined head with a fresh test cache (P-02 strict, D-065 (5)), on the runner; the gate's ceiling is 180 s (D-064 f)" (`engine_role.CommandGate`, `docs/operations/train.md`).
