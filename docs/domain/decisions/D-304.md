**The factory is built in three layers, in three phases, and its infrastructure is agnostic to the program, the method and the account (Daniel 04/11 11:37 and 11:39 BRT, from Falconi's *O Verdadeiro Poder*: "liderança, método de gestão e conhecimento técnico", then "manda bala"; and his note of 11:45: "é extremamente importante que nossa infraestrutura seja agnóstica ao programa rodado nela"; ARQ-REF 4, #267).** Phase 1 is the technical core alone: it executes, verifies, integrates and records work, and never knows why the work exists, how a method breaks it into steps or whose account it runs in (docs/arquitetura-referencia/22). Phase 2 is management (objectives, bets, the planner, the padrão, named steps, the commit plan, policy checks, premises, learning), a program on top of the core and agnostic of leadership; phase 3 is leadership (the intention and its lock, the north-star metric, limits and authority). Acceptance test: two programs with different management methods run on the same infrastructure without one line of it changing. Under infra/, no template names a management or leadership concept, SQL creates only the core schemas (work, agents, verification, integration, events, metrics), and no template fixes an account or a region: an installation passes its values as parameters. The gate's fixed policy stages (structure check, PR metadata) become checks a repository registers, in a later PR under this decision; this repository keeps registering them, so its own enforcement does not change. Not built now: several clients, billing, per-program row policies, an installer for another account. Revert path: revert this record and its test; the bootstrap keeps working with the region of its stack.
