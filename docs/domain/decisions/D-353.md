**Proposed for Daniel's decision (status: proposed; not approved by the owner): adopt eight external, non-self-reported trust indicators and a three-level, scope-calibrated autonomy ladder with automatic demotion circuit breakers (audit C8 and §6).** Rather than treating trust as a single global binary built on voluntary trailers or gitignored local logs, `config/governance/autonomy-ladder.json` (validated against `contracts/model/autonomy.v1.json` and computed by `scripts/model trust` / `scripts/lib/trust_metrics.py`) exposes eight external indicators: (1) main branch full-suite integrity, (2) external change-failure rate across git reverts, errata, full-suite regressions, deploy failures and Fixes-PR plus diagnostic 5-PR line rework, (3) PR first-pass yield and external flake rate, (4) independent audit agreement excluding self-reviewed samples, (5) tracked owner corrective override rate flagging gitignored local paths as unmeasured, (6) C8 product/capacity work share vs. meta-governance overhead and sequential same-scope PR split detection, (7) production deploy latency (p100 acceptance with diagnostic p50/p95/p99 and swap/reload breakdown), synthetic canary and verified gross AWS spend excluding credits (D-303), and (8) security posture refusing session-authorized or proposed TAIL exemptions, open threat findings and unpinned or curl-to-shell Dockerfiles. All quality, reliability, audit, canary, escape, flake, PR-split and security criteria retain the 100% / 0-failure rule of D-337; work-mix bounds in indicator 6 (`value_share >= 0.70`, `meta_governance_share <= 0.15`) are proposed for Daniel's explicit confirmation. Autonomy unlocks gradually by risk scope — Level A (low-risk docs/tests/refactors after 7 continuous days on 1, 2, 3, 8), Level B (`apps/web` and flow optimization after 14 continuous days on all 8), Level C (autonomous unit/backlog generation after 30 continuous days at Level B with zero overrides/gaming/splits) — with automatic demotion to `none` whenever circuit breakers 1, 2 or 8 trip, while intention, stop criteria, trust guards, security TAIL exemptions, GitHub workflows/rulesets and irreversible production/budget actions remain permanently locked to Daniel. While `D-353` stays `proposed` and `autonomy-ladder.json` stays `state: "proposed"`, effective autonomy remains `none`. Revert path: reject `D-353` or supersede it with a later decision of Daniel and remove `config/governance/autonomy-ladder.json` and `scripts/lib/trust_metrics.py`.
