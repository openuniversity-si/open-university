**Every Tower VM job has one budget, `service.job_budget_s = 10 s`, from claim to its definite answer and release of its resources.** Daniel, 02/11 15:58 BRT: "A VM tem 10 segundos para realizar tudo - seja dar positivo e seguir, seja dar negativo e estar liberada para o próximo".

1. The runner starts accounting before the claim request, refreshes before claiming, and claims one job at a time.
   An outer alarm covers head checks, Python work and result reporting. The child gets an execution deadline in
   `TOWER_JOB_DEADLINE` and its owner in `TOWER_JOB_OWNER`; the final two seconds remain inside the budget for cleanup
   and reporting. TERM precedes the execution deadline by one second, KILL enforces it, and descendants are killed
   even after their leader exits. Every outcome carries `reason` and `phases`; an exhausted budget is `over_budget`.
2. The train lease ends at the child's deadline and never renews beyond it. TERM/alarm unwind the role's release;
   the supervisor additionally releases by exact owner after death and on restart. A process generation is recorded
   before work; recovery cannot kill a reused pid. If the forge is unreachable, the supervisor waits for lease expiry
   before calling the job finished. The old independent train lease and runner timeout settings are removed.
3. The control-plane lease derives directly from `BUDGET_S`: reporting takes no extra lease. Every request expires
   dead work, and a lease ending without a result records `died`, clears the runner slot, and never requeues a gate
   or train. A late result cannot overwrite that answer; another runner cannot finish the holder's job.
4. A runner gate job runs `scripts/gate-dev --pr N`, the real stages and per-test content-addressed proofs,
   under the same enforced budget. A missing whole-gate attestation is not a negative substitute for executing the
   gate. The real driver chooses its tooling before one preflight, overlaps independent stages, shares read-only
   Python syntax within a structure run and overlaps independent forge reads and result consumers. Exact input
   keys remain mandatory for reuse; the bypass and whole-gate attestation are never timing evidence for outcome (c).
5. `service.token_holders` names every credential consumer. A missing mandatory environment file stops systemd;
   refused claims, heartbeats, results and journal requests name the stale credential. Result reasons and phase
   times are relayed through the Worker's event log. The coordinator updates every inventory holder on rotation.
6. A base push queues one `deploy` job (GitHub and Origin), ahead of normal work. In that same enforced budget it
   compares `DEPLOYED_TREE`, uploads the merged Worker only when changed, and preserves secret bindings. A failed
   or dead deploy is retried with backoff; only one pending deploy survives newer base pushes. No between-poll
   deploy path remains. Job v2 replaces v1 atomically; every reference and generated type moves with it.
7. Closed/merged PRs cancel queued work and leave a persistent closed barrier against late `pr.ready` or push events;
   only an explicit reopen clears it. Supersession, TTL, lease expiry and runner retirement prevent stale work
   from rebuilding the backlog.
