**Our target is always 100 % (Daniel, 19/11/2026 02:51 BRT, through the Torre, his words quoted: "Nossa meta é sempre 100%.").** Every target, limit and conformance criterion of the standard aims at 100 %: a share, a coverage, a convergence, a conformance or a sample is accepted only when all of it passes. The only objects left out are exemptions that are decided, recorded and in force, each with its reason and its owner (config/standard/tailoring). External scales (the ISO/IEC 33020 levels N, P, L and F, percentiles) only report; they are never an acceptance criterion. It is a rule of the standard that the gate checks: a limit below 100 % with no recorded exemption fails, for example a validation in config/validation whose comparator is gte with a threshold below 1 on a share, a registry key that sets a percentile below 100 for an acceptance, or a conformance level accepted below full. It applies at once to the three slacks of 19/11 02:45 BRT (D-336), and it supersedes D-336's choice of the 99th percentile for the SLOs: an SLO judges its whole sample (the maximum), and p95 and p99 stay as reported values. D-335 already makes full conformance the stop criterion; this decision makes it the rule for every threshold. Reason: a threshold that forgives a share of the failures lets an object or a system pass with failures inside it, and a team that fixes in minutes has no use for that slack. The proof is the gate's check of thresholds in scripts/model check, tested in scripts/tests/test_model.py. Revert path: a later decision of Daniel that supersedes this one.
