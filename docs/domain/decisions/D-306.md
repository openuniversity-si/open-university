**The boundary between infrastructure and management (Daniel 04/11 12:19 BRT, approving the consolidation of his words of 12:05 to 12:17: "Consolida sim [...] porque eu acho importante essa separação"; "Aqui eu tô olhando trabalho, bicho. Trabalho."; amends D-304 and D-305; ARQ-REF 4, #267).** There are three systems: leadership says what is wanted, management turns it into defined work, and the infrastructure executes and records work without knowing why. The infrastructure knows only work: the job (an id, an opaque input, a lifecycle state); the executor plugged in by an adapter, an AI session being one kind, so that running a script never opens one; the output as the executor produced it; the raw log of all it observes; full access to that log, as a subscribable stream and as history; and the physical properties every model has: isolation between tenants, durability, security and the limit mechanism. It does not know, and keeps no field for, goals, objectives, requesters, projects, programs in the management sense, priority, hierarchy, which information matters (time, cost and success are data in the log that management chooses to read), metrics, dashboards, alerts, reports, billing, which executor to use and when, or who may create work. The infrastructure offers mechanisms; whoever uses it decides values and reasons. Amends D-304 and D-305: the schemas of the infrastructure are work and log, with the orchestrator's dbos, and no SQL under infra/ names a context field, a chosen measure or a management concept. Acceptance test: replace the whole management system, or the execution behaviour, without changing a line of the infrastructure, and run the same job as a script and as an AI session (docs/arquitetura-referencia/22 §6). Revert path: revert this record and the schema list of its test.
