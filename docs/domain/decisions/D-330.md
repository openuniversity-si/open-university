**PADRAO-DEV, management is its own group of systems: the model's systems split into a management group and an engineering group, and docs/padrao/METODO.md is the current standard of the management group (Daniel, 18/11/2026 20:31 BRT, through the Torre: "manda é pra esperar meu ok em nada não. Meu ok é pra ser esperado situações muito específicas, normalmente quando envolve dinheiro ou outras pessoas.", with the Torre's instruction to proceed with the session's recommendation for draft C; D-rascunho-C of docs/programs/padrao-dev/regras-sem-decisao.md, reshaped by his question of 18/11 20:19 BRT on how to manage management).** Daniel sees management as a group of systems apart from engineering. The boundary follows ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288's own split between the technical processes and the management ones: the management group holds the systems whose objects are the work and its direction, not the engine: intent (business or mission analysis, the SEMENTE), work (portfolio management, project planning, project assessment and control), decisions (decision management) and measurement (measurement). The other thirteen systems are the engineering group, whose standard stays the rule catalog of config/standard/rules. METODO.md (four levels programa, projeto, processo, operação; PDCA for programa and projeto, SDCA for processo and operação; one name, retrospectiva) is the management group's standard as it stands today, not a decision of its own: it changes by a PR to that document, judged like any standard, and the group may replace it whole; where it differs from Padrão v7/v8 or from the unit flow of D-077, it prevails within its group (D-292 already dropped mandatory intake). This decision fixes only the boundary and which document is the group's standard. What changes: STD-metodo takes this decision as its basis; contracts/model/system.v1.json gains the field group (management or engineering, an additive field), each system file names its group and scripts/model reports each group's levels apart, in their own PR, whose test in scripts/tests/test_model.py proves the split (pending: no case of it exists yet). Reason: the management of the flow and the engineering of the engine have different objects, owners and cycles (PDCA/SDCA against life-cycle processes), and measuring them as one pool hides a management system at N behind engineering systems at F; the reference standard already draws that line, so the boundary needs no invention. Revert path: a later decision that supersedes this one; the systems return to one pool and STD-metodo to no basis.
