**PADRAO-DEV, the rules no decision states: every record the engine reads to decide conforms to its contract, and the module that owns the record refuses one that does not (Daniel, 18/11/2026 20:19 BRT, through the Torre: "pode aprovar, pode seguir com isso aí"; D-rascunho-A of docs/programs/padrao-dev/regras-sem-decisao.md).** A record is what the engine writes and later reads to judge: a registry value, a baseline, a unit's census event, a lesson, a catalog, a program's plan block, a log a store receives. Its contract is the JSON Schema that declares it plus the invariants that schema language cannot state: a field present exactly when another holds, a closed vocabulary kept as a registry list (D-068), a path or name it cites that must exist. Those invariants are written once, in the module that owns the record, next to the schema they complete, and a test names each one. The owner refuses on write and the reader refuses on read; nothing is judged on a record that fails its contract. Reason: six seeded rules (STD-budgets-schema-errors, STD-census-shape-errors, STD-lessons-vocabulary-errors, STD-vendor-check-catalog-errors, STD-retro-shape-errors, STD-program-plan-block) protect the same thing, and the reference practice (one compatibility policy for a schema registry, the Kubernetes CRD schema with its CEL rules beside it, referential integrity as a property of the model) treats it as one principle; D-069 items 2 and 3 give the reason but state compatibility and registry values, not each record checked against its contract. One decision for the class, never one per schema: a new record type gets its contract and its check under this rule, and its rule in config/standard/rules/ takes this decision as its basis. What changes: the six rules take this decision as their basis; their statements, applies_to and the census invariants' test follow in their own PRs. The proof is the test of scripts/model (a rule's basis is a recorded decision). Revert path: a later decision that supersedes this one; its rules return to no basis.
