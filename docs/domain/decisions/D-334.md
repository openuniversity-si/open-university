**PADRAO-DEV, the fast deploy's propagation probe is a measurement, not a state-wait (decided by the session under D-325, at the Torre's request of 19/11 00:15 BRT; reported to Daniel, who may supersede it).** fastdeploy.propagate invokes the Lambda alias until it answers the new version, at most deploy.fast_propagation_s, and records propagated and propagation_ms; the drills use it to measure how long an alias swap takes to route. AWS publishes no event when an alias's routing converges (UpdateAlias returns at once and its CloudTrail record is the call, not the convergence), so no event can replace it, and the alternative of dropping the probe would remove the only measure of that time and the smoke of the swapped alias. Its POLL record moves from the class state-wait to measurement, which VAL-events-no-polling does not count; the probe stays bounded by its registry key and the drills keep reading its result. Cheapest path: a reclassification with its reason, not new infrastructure for an event AWS does not emit. Revert path: a later decision that supersedes this one, if AWS publishes such an event or a cheaper probe appears.
