**PADRAO-DEV, no threshold accepts less than the whole where the whole is reachable (decided by the session under D-325, at the Torre's request of 19/11 02:45 BRT carrying Daniel's concern; reported to him, who may supersede it; it applies D-335).** Three thresholds tolerated a part of the failures and are tightened. (1) VAL-changeability requires 100 % of the architecture's dependencies convergent, counting a divergence as convergent only when a decision records it as accepted; it was 85 %. The reflexion already reads 100 %, so nothing breaks, and an accepted divergence stays visible by its decision. (2) A vulnerability of the engine's own code (a VULN of config/security/vulns) or an advisory affecting a dependency counts as conformant only when it is fixed, or when a decision accepts it with its reason and an expiry; an open one inside its deadline is no longer conformant. The deadlines of config/registry/security (7, 30, 90 and 180 days by severity) stay as the alarm that a fix is late, not as a licence to stay open: a team that fixes in minutes needs no slack to be conformant, and a fix that waits on an upstream release is the case acceptance exists for. (3) The SLOs of config/registry/slo judge the 99th percentile instead of the 95th (slo.percentile 95 → 99), by nearest rank, so on a sample under 100 the 99th percentile is its maximum. The maximum alone was rejected: one runner hiccup would decide every window, and p99 already equals it on today's samples. Reason: under D-335 a system closes only when every object passes, and a threshold that forgives a share of the failures would let a system close with failures inside it. The proof is the model's reading of each threshold in scripts/tests/test_model.py. Revert path: a later decision that supersedes this one; the three thresholds return to 85 %, open-within-deadline and p95.
