**Every choice of how to solve a problem starts from the cheapest effort that solves it, asked at decision time and never as a field that blocks a PR (Daniel, 18/11/2026 19:24 BRT, approved 20:19 BRT, through the Torre: "pode aprovar, pode seguir com isso aí"; D-rascunho-F of docs/programs/padrao-dev/regras-sem-decisao.md).** Before a session chooses how to do something (a design, a fix, a delegation, an AWS change), it names the cheapest path that would solve it and takes a costlier one only for a reason the measure or the risk gives, which it states in one line where the choice is recorded: the commit message or PR body that carries it, or the brief that delegates it. Three places carry it: (1) AGENTS.md's judgment rules, read at the start of every session, state it next to D-294; (2) every brief to a subagent names the cheapest acceptable path and what is out of scope, because a delegated task spends most of the effort; (3) the retrospective samples merged PRs, 10 per retrospective, and asks of each "was there a clearly cheaper path that solved it?"; the share of yes is a measure, with a target under 10 % reviewed after three retrospectives, so the rule is checked by its effect, not by a filled field.
