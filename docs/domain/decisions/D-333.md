**A threat model counts as reviewed when a reviewer that did not write it records its findings and the review date (decided by the session under D-325, reported to Daniel through the Torre; he may supersede it).** D-055 keeps the delivery free of a review step for PRs; a threat model is different, because SSDF PW.2 asks that the design be reviewed against its threats, and no machine check can say a list of threats is complete. The reviewer is a separate agent given only the review brief and the files, not the author's conclusions; it records, in each THREAT record, the reviewer, the date and every finding with its disposition (fixed, accepted with reason, or open). A finding left open keeps the model under review. The machine check of scripts/tests/test_security.py still holds every model to its structure. Reason: a model judged only by its author is a hypothesis, and faking a review date would make the security measure lie; an independent read is the cheapest review that is real. Revert path: a later decision that supersedes this one; the models return to written.
