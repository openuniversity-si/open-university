**The engineering model starts at the problem, not at the need: the intent system gains PROB (a problem, opportunity or threat with its current state and evidence, refining an intention) and OBJ (an objective measured against a baseline and a target, addressing a problem, its state derived from its measure as the benefit reading), and every need derives from an objective (Daniel's question of 19/11 ~13:11 BRT, relayed by the Torre; decided by the session under D-325).** The research (docs/programs/padrao-dev/problema-pesquisa.md) found the consensus of ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023 6.4.1-6.4.2, 29148:2018, the INCOSE Needs and Requirements Manual, BABOK v3 and ISO 21502:2020 7.3: a problem or opportunity comes first, then objectives with measures, a baseline and a target, then the chosen solution and its concept, then the needs and the requirements; validation proves the needs and the benefit measured after delivery proves the objectives, which closes the loop back to the portfolio and the intention. The audit (problema-desenho.md) found no problem object, no baseline and needs that mix objectives with needs about a solution. The cheapest path keeps one system: PROB and OBJ live in config/intent/ beside INT and NEED, their rules are the metamodel's coverage rules (INT covered by a PROB, PROB by an OBJ, OBJ by a NEED; must_link refines, addresses, derives-from), and an OBJ reads the same measures as a validation. Root causes, options, experiments and concepts stay in the problem's text, the decisions, the premises and the program documents until a measure asks for objects. This is a new unit and does not reopen D-331. Revert path: delete the PROB and OBJ files and types and restore INT's coverage by NEED.
