The exception regime is a recorded state, not a message: registry key regime.exception (active, granted_by, granted_at, reason, exit, kept). While it holds, every lock of the engine lifts through one question, regime.lifted(lock), and a lifted lock warns and emits lock.lifted instead of refusing; gate.bypass becomes one case of it. Two locks never lift: money, and other people (production, messages, others' repositories, secrets, history of main and the base).
