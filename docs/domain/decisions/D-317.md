**PADRAO-DEV: the engineering standard is one model of 15 managed systems, and the program ends at 100 % of the chain verified (Daniel, 15/11/2026 19:50, 20:42, 20:44 and 21:12 BRT, through the Torre's session).** Daniel opened the program of the internal development standard (19:50), corrected its first solution as too focused on tests (20:42: requirements, architecture and the standard each a unified system) and set the rule that everything has a system (20:44); at 21:12 he approved revision 2 (PR #61) with the stop criterion at 100 %. The solution (docs/programs/padrao-dev/solucao.md) is a model of the engineering life cycle kept in this repository: the metamodel as data in config/model/ (one file per object type, link type, system and inventory source, each checked against contracts/model/), one file per object in the authoritative source of each system, links that record the target's fingerprint and turn suspect when it changes, states derived from links, and conformance calculated per system on the N/P/L/F scale (ISO/IEC 33020); scripts/model checks, shows and measures it. The 15 systems are the 30 processes of ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023 and 12207:2017 grouped by managed object: intent, requirements, architecture, decisions, work, construction and delivery, verification, validation, configuration, operation, measurement, conformance, standard, standard management, knowledge. The main metric is the share of active requirements whose chain (requirement, architecture element, unit, test, validation) is verified end to end; the guards are the requirement inventory (no source left out), the escape rate (10.9 % at the baseline), throughput, the gate's 60 s, AWS under US$ 200 a month and the 1 s median deploy. Invariant: the program stops only when the chain covers 100 % of the active requirement inventory, all 15 systems rate F, the escape rate is under 1 % over at least 1,000 merged PRs and no guard is worse than its baseline, or when a measured physical limit stops it; a requirement that cannot be verified is verified or retired by a decision, never left out. The proof is the test of scripts/model, which keeps the metamodel valid and computes the chain, the inventory and the conformance. Revert path: a later decision that supersedes this one.
