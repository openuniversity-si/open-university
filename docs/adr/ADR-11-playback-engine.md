# ADR-11 playback engine: YouTube in production (v02)

Status: deprecated by D-111 (the Open University product left this repository; PADRAO-DEV F3a).

The production build resolves `@tower/playback-engine` to `src/playback/engine/youtube.ts`: the core state machine over `YouTubeTransport` (`src/playback/youtube-engine.ts`), which loads the IFrame Player API on intent or on the first gesture and plays the lesson's `youtubeId` through a `www.youtube.com` embed (never `youtube-nocookie.com`: it stops playing when the screen locks on Opera Android, 2026-10-11; locked by a unit test and by the CSP `frame-src`); `pnpm build:test` still resolves to the fake engine, and `bundle:check` proves production contains only `youtube` (no `fake`). YouTube policy: there is no "Watch on YouTube" chip (Daniel, v02); once the video plays, the embed's own title, logo and overlay show and stay clickable, and no essential click is left without an alternative.

Controls are YouTube's own ("nativo total", Daniel, 10 Oct 2026): the embed runs with `controls=1`, `rel=0`, `playsinline=1`, `iv_load_policy=3` and YouTube's keyboard shortcuts (no `disablekb`), and the iframe's `allow` list includes `fullscreen`. Play/pause, the progress bar, speed, captions and full screen are all native; The Tower draws no scrubber, ±10 s, speed menu, full-screen button, time, pause or page shortcuts, and no progress bar of its own anywhere. Ours, over the lesson's opaque poster: the glass play button (idle only), the loading spinner and the error panel with Try again. Progress is recorded behind the scenes from the IFrame API (`getCurrentTime` every 250 ms while playing, `onStateChange`): 15 s checkpoints, pause, hide/pagehide, completion at the end, resume from the saved second or `?t=` (`start`), so % watched, Continue Watching and Mark complete work as before. A play, replay or pause the viewer makes on YouTube's controls reaches the engine: `onStateChange` PLAYING with no attempt of ours is adopted as a new attempt (`onExternalPlay`), and a media-side pause only records the state (the engine never pauses the video again). Speed is not tracked: it doesn't affect the position the progress is built from.

Poster rule (one rule, no separate native mode): the poster covers the embed (the iframe stays hidden) until the embed starts, i.e. until the engine first reaches `playing`, or `paused` before it ever played. After our play click the transport calls `playVideo()`; if the player isn't PLAYING `HANDOFF_MS` (3 s) later, it hands the play to YouTube's controls by reporting `paused` without stopping the video: the poster lifts, so on iOS (which refuses `playVideo()` from outside the iframe) the viewer taps YouTube's play, and a slow start carries on behind YouTube's spinner and is adopted when it plays. 3 s rather than a longer wait because iOS refuses every time, so the viewer would otherwise stare at our spinner; the cost of lifting early on a slow network is only YouTube's own spinner showing. From then on the embed is the picture, also at the end (YouTube's end screen with replay). An error before the first play keeps the poster under the error panel.

Mux is removed, not paused (D-062, 2026-10-26). Its engine, token signing and probes were deleted with the switch to YouTube (`L3.vendor-retired` refuses a declaration or an import of `@mux/*` or `media-chrome` anywhere in `apps/web`), and roadmap step 5 removed `@mux/mux-video`, `media-chrome` and the e2e signing variables. A lesson video's provider is `youtube`. Going back to Mux is a new decision and a new engine, not a revert.
